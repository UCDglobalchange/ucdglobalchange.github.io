# Graduate Milestones

Graduate requirements depend on your graduate group. Lab students usually belong to one of:

- **[Geography Graduate Group](https://geography.ucdavis.edu)**
  ([Graduate Studies program page](https://grad.ucdavis.edu/programs/ggeo))
- **[Atmospheric Science Graduate Group](https://atm.ucdavis.edu)**

The **Soils and Biogeochemistry** graduate group may also become an option in the future.

**Always check your graduate group's handbook** for its exact requirements, timeline, and forms,
and talk to your graduate program coordinator. The timeline below is typical, not official.

## Typical PhD timeline

| Year | Milestones |
|------|-----------|
| 1 | Coursework; choose a research direction; form your committee; start an Individual Development Plan |
| 2 | Finish core courses; first paper underway; prepare for the **Qualifying Exam (QE)** |
| 2–3 | Pass the QE and **advance to candidacy** |
| 3–5 | Research, papers, conferences; annual committee meetings |
| Final | Write and file the dissertation; exit seminar/defense as required by your group |

## Typical MS timeline

Coursework plus a thesis (Plan I) or comprehensive exam (Plan II), usually over two years.

## The Qualifying Exam

- Start preparing **at least 6 months** ahead; discuss committee membership with the PI.
- Prepare a written research proposal if your group requires it.
- Give a **practice QE** at lab meeting and do mock oral exams with lab mates.
- Review fundamentals from your core courses; the committee will test breadth as well as your project.

## Individual Development Plan (IDP)

Each year, write or update an IDP covering your research goals, skills to develop, and career
plans, and discuss it with the PI. A free tool: [myIDP](https://myidp.sciencecareers.org).

## Fellowships

Apply early and often: NSF GRFP (first or second year), NASA FINESST, DOE CSGF/SCGSR, and UC Davis
fellowships through Graduate Studies (<https://grad.ucdavis.edu>).
