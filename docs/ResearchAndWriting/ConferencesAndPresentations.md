# Conferences and Presentations

## Conferences we attend

The lab's main conference is the **[AGU Fall Meeting](https://www.agu.org/annual-meeting)**,
held every December. It is the largest Earth and space science meeting in the world, and most lab
members present there.

- **Abstract deadline**: usually late July or early August. Plan your abstract with the PI in early
  summer.
- **Format**: oral or poster; you request a preference when you submit, and conveners decide.
- **Student support**: AGU offers student travel grants and Outstanding Student Presentation
  Awards (OSPA). Apply for both.

Other meetings and workshops may be relevant to particular projects; discuss them with the PI.

Abstract deadlines are typically **4–6 months before** a meeting. Discuss plans and funding with the PI before submitting
([Travel and Reimbursements](../NewMemberResources/TravelAndReimbursements.md)). Look for student
travel grants offered by the conference.

## Practice talks

Everyone gives a **practice talk at lab meeting** before a conference presentation, qualifying exam,
or job talk. Schedule it at least a week ahead so there is time to revise.
