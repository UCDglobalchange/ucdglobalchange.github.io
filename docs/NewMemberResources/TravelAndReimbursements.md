# Travel, Budgeting, and Reimbursements

## Before you travel

1. **Ask the PI first.** Get the PI's confirmation that you can attend **and** that your travel
   expenses will be reimbursed, before you register or book anything. The PI will tell you which
   grant covers the trip.
2. Keep the PI's approval (e.g., the Slack message or email) with your trip records.
3. Book reasonably priced travel, and **keep every receipt** (registration, airfare, lodging,
   ground transportation, meals as allowed).
4. For international travel, also follow UC's travel registration requirements.

## Getting reimbursed: Aggie Expense

Submit your expense report in **[Aggie Expense](https://us2.concursolutions.com/home.asp)**, UC
Davis's travel and expense system (Concur), **soon after you return**, since UC has deadlines for
reimbursement.

Include:

- itemized receipts for every expense,
- proof of the trip's purpose (conference registration confirmation, agenda, or program),
- the funding source the PI gave you.

If you're unsure whether something is reimbursable, ask the PI **before** you spend the money.

## Purchases

Ask the PI before purchasing anything for your research (software, equipment, data, supplies).
Purchases are charged to a specific grant, so they need the PI's approval first.

## Funding opportunities for students

- UC Davis Graduate Studies awards and travel funding: <https://grad.ucdavis.edu>
- Graduate-group travel awards (check with your graduate group).
- **AGU student travel grants** for the Fall Meeting (see
  [Conferences and Presentations](../ResearchAndWriting/ConferencesAndPresentations.md)).
