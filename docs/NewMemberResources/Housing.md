# Housing in Davis

Davis is a small college town, and housing fills up early, so start looking as soon as you know
you're coming.

## Where to look

- UC Davis Student Housing and Dining Services (including graduate and family housing):
  <https://housing.ucdavis.edu>
- Off-campus housing listings and resources from UC Davis.
- Word of mouth: ask lab members and your graduate group's mailing list.

## Tips

- The main leasing season for the next academic year is roughly in the winter and early spring.
- Most of Davis is bikeable, and Unitrans buses are free with a student ID.
- Read leases carefully and know your rights as a tenant; UC Davis offers tenant resources.
