## Graduate program requirements

Required courses depend on your graduate group: see the
[Geography](https://grad.ucdavis.edu/programs/ggeo) and
[Atmospheric Science](https://atm.ucdavis.edu) graduate groups
([Graduate Milestones](../ResearchAndWriting/GraduateMilestones.md)). Discuss your course plan with
the PI and your graduate advisor each year.
