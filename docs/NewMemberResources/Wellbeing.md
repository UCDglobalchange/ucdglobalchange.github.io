# Wellbeing and Support

Your health and wellbeing come first. Graduate school is demanding, and asking for help is a sign
of good judgment, not weakness. If you are struggling, please talk to the PI, a lab mate, or one of
the resources below.

## Campus resources

- **Student Health and Counseling Services (SHCS)**, including counseling and crisis support:
  <https://shcs.ucdavis.edu>
- **Graduate Studies** wellness and support resources: <https://grad.ucdavis.edu>
- **Ombuds Office** — confidential, neutral help with conflicts: <https://ombuds.ucdavis.edu>
- **Harassment & Discrimination Assistance and Prevention Program (HDAPP)**: <https://hdapp.ucdavis.edu>
- **Student Disability Center** for accommodations: <https://sdc.ucdavis.edu>
- **Aggie Compass Basic Needs Center** (food, housing, financial help): <https://aggiecompass.ucdavis.edu>

```{admonition} In an emergency
:class: danger
Call **911**. For a mental-health crisis, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
```

## In the lab

- Take time off and use your vacation; tell the lab when you're away.
- Set reasonable working hours; we don't expect late-night or weekend emails to be answered.
