# Welcome to the Lab

We are very glad you are here. This page describes how the lab works day to day. Read it in your
first week and come back to it whenever you have questions.

## Mission and values

<!-- TODO: edit to reflect the lab's own mission statement and values. -->
Our mission is to do rigorous, open, and useful science on global environmental change, and to
train the next generation of scientists in a supportive environment. We value:

- **Curiosity and rigor**: ask good questions and check your work carefully.
- **Openness**: share code, data, and ideas; science is better when it is reproducible.
- **Respect and inclusion**: everyone in the lab deserves a welcoming, harassment-free workplace.
- **Well-being**: sustainable work habits produce better science than heroics.

## First steps

The [First-Week Checklist](FirstWeekChecklist.md) lists every account and task to complete when
you arrive. In short, you will need to:

- get your UC Davis computing account (Kerberos login) and email set up;
- join the lab **Slack** workspace, the lab **Box** folder, and the lab calendar;
- join the lab GitHub organization, [UCDglobalchange](https://github.com/UCDglobalchange) ([Git and GitHub](../CodingAndData/GitAndGithub.md));
- request a FARM account ([Getting Started with FARM](../Computing/GettingStartedWithFARM.md)).

## Lab roles

Running the lab is shared work: lab members manage areas such as Slack, the Box folder, GitHub,
FARM, lab meetings, and this manual. See
[Lab Responsibilities and Who to Contact](../NewMemberResources/Who_to_contact_for.md) to find who is
in charge of what.

## Meetings

**Lab meetings** happen **every week**. <!-- TODO: day/time/room or Zoom link --> Members take
turns presenting research updates, practice talks, or papers. Everyone is expected to attend and
participate.

**Individual (1:1) meetings** with the PI are either a **30-minute meeting every week** or a
**1-hour meeting every other week**; agree with the PI on which works best for you. Come with an
agenda: what you did, what went well, where you are stuck, and what you plan next. Keep a running
meeting-notes document in your Box folder that both of you can see.

Talk to the PI or the department office about desk space, building access, and keys. Lab-funded
computers and equipment remain university property.

## Work hours and time off

We value results and well-being, not hours at a desk. Agree with the PI on a schedule that works
for you, be reachable during normal working hours, and let the lab know in advance when you will be
away (vacation, conferences, fieldwork). Follow UC Davis and your appointment's policies on
vacation and sick leave.

## Presentations, conferences, and publishing

- Practice talks are given at lab meeting before every conference presentation.
- Discuss conference plans with the PI early, since travel requires funding approval
  (see [Travel and Reimbursements](../NewMemberResources/TravelAndReimbursements.md)).
- Authorship is discussed openly at the start of a project.
- Code and data supporting publications should be archived in a public repository
  (e.g., GitHub + Zenodo) whenever possible.
