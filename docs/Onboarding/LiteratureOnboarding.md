# Literature Onboarding

Reading widely is the fastest way to get up to speed. This page lists foundational references and
tips for keeping up with the literature.

## Managing references

Use a reference manager from day one. [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org) is free, open source, works
with Word, Google Docs and LaTeX (via the Better BibTeX plugin), and supports shared group
libraries.

## Discovering papers

- **Google Scholar** and **Web of Science** for searching and citation tracking.
- **Connected Papers** and **Research Rabbit** to map related work from a seed paper.
- **Semantic Scholar** and **Elicit** for AI-assisted search; always verify what they return
  (see [AI Tools](../CodingAndData/AITools.md)).

## Accessing papers

Use the [UC Davis Library](https://library.ucdavis.edu). Off campus, connect through the UC Davis
VPN or the library proxy to get full-text access.

## Keeping up

- Set up Google Scholar alerts for key authors and topics.
- Follow table-of-contents alerts for journals in the field (e.g., *Nature Climate Change*,
  *Journal of Climate*, *Earth's Future*, *Environmental Research Letters*, *GMD*).
- Share interesting papers in the lab chat; we discuss papers regularly at lab meeting.
