# Organization and Scheduling

Graduate school is a long project with many parallel threads. Good habits help.

## Planning your time

- At the start of each quarter, list your courses, TA duties, research goals, and deadlines.
- Keep a **Gantt chart** for each project or paper (a spreadsheet works fine).
- Set weekly goals and review them before your individual meeting with the PI.
- Block focused time for research in your calendar, and protect it.

## Notes and knowledge management

Keep a research log: what you ran, why, where the output is, and what you learned. Options:

- **Obsidian** — local Markdown notes with links between notes.
- **Notion** — web-based, good for shared lab pages and to-do lists.
- **OneNote / Google Docs** — simple, shareable.
- A Markdown `NOTES.md` in each project repository.

## Meeting notes

Keep one running document in your Box folder, shared with the PI, for your individual meetings: date, what you did,
questions, decisions, and next steps.

## Tasks

Any to-do system you'll actually use works: Todoist, Things, Microsoft To Do, GitHub Issues for
code, or paper and pen.
